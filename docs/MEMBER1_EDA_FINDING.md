The sender balance consistency analysis revealed a substantial difference between fraudulent and legitimate transactions. Approximately 99.5% of fraudulent TRANSFER and CASH_OUT transactions followed the expected sender balance equation. In contrast, legitimate transactions exhibited significantly higher mismatch rates across transaction types. This indicates that balance inconsistency alone may not be an effective fraud indicator in the PaySim dataset.
